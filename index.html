Who we are

MediByte Technologies is a healthcare BPO and revenue cycle management organization specializing in Medical Coding, Medical Billing, Quality Assurance, AR Calling, and Healthcare Analytics.

We partner with healthcare providers, hospitals, physician groups, clinics, and RCM companies across the globe to improve financial performance, ensure regulatory compliance, and optimize operational efficiency.
