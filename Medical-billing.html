What is Medical Billing?

Medical billing is the process of translating healthcare services into insurance claims and ensuring healthcare providers receive accurate and timely payment for the services they deliver. It is a vital part of the healthcare revenue cycle, connecting patients, healthcare providers, and insurance companies. The medical billing process begins after a patient receives treatment. Medical billers review medical documentation, verify insurance eligibility, prepare and submit claims, monitor claim status, resolve claim denials, post payments, and follow up on outstanding balances. Accurate medical billing helps healthcare organizations reduce claim rejections, improve cash flow, and maintain financial stability. Professional medical billing services also ensure compliance with healthcare regulations, insurance policies, and industry standards, minimizing billing errors and maximizing reimbursement.
